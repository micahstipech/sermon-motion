# What reads as AI-made (and as "every church channel")

Check every mood board and build against this list. Changing the hex while keeping the pattern does
not count. Colour patterns from huashu-motion's measured study of launch films (MIT); copy rules from
coreyhaines31/marketingskills (MIT).

## Colour

- Saturated colour on black or near-black for "contrast" (the brief's first no).
- Dark ground + one warm accent (orange, amber, terracotta, vermilion) + cream/bone type.
- Cream ground + serif + terracotta accent.
- Near-black + acid lime/green; neon on dark; purple-to-blue gradients; teal-orange grade looks.
- Medium saturation everywhere (mud). Saturation should live in one place.
- An accent on every element.
- Tinted type (cream, bone, beige). Type is neutral.
- Flat full-screen colour fields that are not from the art.

## Type

- Default faces with no reason (Inter/Geist/Montserrat-on-everything) when the series has its own.
- Two display faces together; more than two families.
- Huge all-caps heavy grotesk "slams" for reflective content.
- Tight leading on multi-line verses; centred everything; widows of one short word.
- Text in a solid lower-third bar or pill (unless it is the church's brand asset).

## Motion

- Blur-in as the default entrance (`focus` has a budget of one per video).
- Every element scales up from 0, pops, or overshoots.
- Constant slow zoom/drift on text; floating; ken-burns on type.
- Per-letter cascades everywhere; typewriter text for scripture.
- Identical entrance on every graphic; identical intro/outro every week.
- Particles, bokeh, light leaks, glows, shine sweeps, "cinematic" flares, glitch.
- Transitions as decoration (whip pans, spins, zoom-throughs) between graphics.

## Copy on screen

- "It's not X, it's Y." "No X, no Y, no Z." Colon reveals ("The answer: grace."). Self-answered
  questions, unless it is the preacher's own question.
- Stock phrases: "unlock", "reimagined", "the power of", "journey", "level up", "game-changer".
- Em dashes as decoration; emoji; exclamation marks the preacher did not say.
- Paraphrasing the preacher into slogans. Use their words.

## Layout

- Every graphic in the same place.
- Graphics over the face or the hands when the hands are speaking.
- Corner logo bugs on every graphic (brand appears where the brand belongs).
